What's new in v2 (reviewer notes)
All 6 required surfaces, all clickable:
  • Group tabs — the ‹ button collapses the sibling strip into a "🗂 N ›" pill; when expanded, drag the thin bar under the tabs (or the tabs themselves) to swipe through all agents in the group.
  • Reminders — gear ▸ Folder reminders, or the 🔔 chip. Configure tab: cadence chips (15/30/45/60/90/custom), Stop-after-N vs Unlimited, per-agent include/skip. Active tab: folder card + per-agent breakdown with live next-fire countdown, plus a "Fire now (demo)" button so you don't have to wait real minutes.
  • Wakeups — ⏰ chip on the header. Quick-picks include real short demo timers (30s/2m actually fire) plus manual date+time. Pending chip clears the moment it fires and drops a message into the transcript.
  • Summaries — Σ chip opens a scrollable, timestamped dropdown per agent. A reminder fire auto-appends a new one.
  • Summary-LLM endpoints — gear ▸ Summary-LLM endpoints. Named endpoints (name/base URL/model), radio-select default, "140" pre-set as default; add/remove more.
  • Draft kept — type in the composer, switch agents via a tab or the scrubber, switch back: your text is restored and a small "✎ Draft kept" pill fades in. Tabs with an unsent draft also carry a small dot.
